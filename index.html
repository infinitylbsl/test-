Digital Banking Services

Infinity Laghubhitta Bittiya Sanstha Ltd. is the first Laghubhitta offering SMS Banking and Mobile Banking, making banking convenient, quick, and accessible from mobile devices. Popular services include Missed-Call Banking and Mobile Banking.

Missed-Call Banking

Recharge your mobile (Ncell, NTC, postpaid) and check your balance easily using a missed call. Designed for customers with non-smartphones—no apps required. Infinity aims to develop cashless banking through M-Banking.

Mobile Banking

Conduct payments, account inquiries, loan payments, internet bills, school fees, and more safely from your mobile. Withdraw funds to digital wallets like eSewa, Khalti, and IME Pay.

Digital Wallets

Deposit balances or pay loans through eSewa, Khalti, NCHL, and Fonepay QR for secure cashless transactions.
