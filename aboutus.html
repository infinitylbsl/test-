Our Purpose

Building financial empowerment and social development across Nepal

Objective

ILBSL aims to empower Nepal’s underserved communities by fostering self-reliance and independence, contributing to rural social development through self-employment and enhanced social awareness.

Vision

To build a sustainable and inclusive society by promoting economic independence among Nepal’s marginalized populations, primarily through self-employment and social empowerment.

Mission

To provide sustainable microfinance services to needy and underserved families across Nepal, supporting their journey toward financial stability and growth.

Goals

  • Empower Deprived Households: Reach the maximum number of financially capable households using proven delivery mechanisms and technology-driven solutions.
  • Institutional Excellence: Build a well-managed, reputable organization with motivated staff committed to achieving organizational objectives.
  • Formalize the Informal Financial Sector: Educate individuals currently outside the banking system and equip them to perform formal banking transactions.
  • Promote Financial Literacy and Inclusion: Enhance access to banking services for marginalized and backward communities.
  • Support Entrepreneurship: Encourage and assist marginalized individuals in establishing Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) to achieve economic independence.
  • Strengthen Rural Economies: Empower rural communities with financial guidance, enabling them to initiate enterprises and generate sustainable income.

Gyanishor Panta

Chief Executive Officer

At Infinity Laghubitta Bittiya Sanstha Limited (ILBSL), our mission is clear: to bridge the gap in financial inclusion for Nepal’s rural and marginalized communities. In an era defined by global economic shifts and local regulatory evolution, ILBSL remains a steadfast partner in fostering economic stability and livelihood enhancement.

Navigating Challenges with Innovation

The microfinance landscape currently faces unique pressures from inflationary trends to a shifting regulatory environment. At ILBSL, we view these challenges as catalysts for strategic transformation. We are prioritizing digitization through mobile banking and digital lending to ensure our services are seamless, cost-effective, and accessible to all.

Commitment to Sustainable Growth

Our strategy is built on three core pillars:

  • Digital Excellence: Leveraging technology to enhance operational resilience and client experience.
  • ESG Integration: Embedding Environmental, Social, and Governance principles into our core operations for long-term value creation.
  • Talent Development: Investing in a skilled, empowered workforce to lead the sector forward.

A Future of Empowerment

As we expand our customer-centric products and strengthen our national branch network, I extend my deepest gratitude to the Government of Nepal, Nepal Rastra Bank, our Board of Directors, and our dedicated staff.

As CEO, I am honored to lead ILBSL into this next chapter. Together, we are creating pathways to lasting prosperity and empowering communities across Nepal.
